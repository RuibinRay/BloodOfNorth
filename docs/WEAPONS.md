## Eirik's skills from the lightning

The story gives him four gifts: the scar, Raven's Voice, Raven's Eye and Stormguard. The **Stormborn** skills below are the game's extension of them, asked for by the owner on 2026-11-12 ("skills like a thunder smash"). All of them, and Stormguard, are paid for from one **storm bar** that fills as blows land and are parried, so using one leaves less for the others.

**Stormguard** (key Z, and on its own). When a blow would leave him near death, the scar burns and the blow is turned, without a key press. Z calls it early for a blow that cannot be blocked. The story's rules are the game's rules: it answers only a calm man (every blow he throws feeds his anger, and the bar turns red; holding the guard brings it back); it costs half the bar the first time and more each time in one fight; it takes a third of his breath and leaves him slow for two seconds; it turns one arrow he can see coming, not arrows from every side; it guards no one else. In the last part of the last fight the scar goes cold and it is gone.

| Skill | Key | Storm bar | Points | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Thunder Smash** | 4 | 40 | his from the day he is grown | Leap and bring the axe down. Lightning follows it: everyone within five paces is hurt, guards break, shield walls open, and a swing that has started is stopped. |
| **Storm-Step** | 5 | 25 | 1 | Go through the man in front of you between one heartbeat and the next, cutting as you pass, and come out behind him. Nothing can touch you on the way. |
| **The Turning Blow** | heavy blow after a Stormguard or parry | — | 2 | Bjorn's log-splitting turn. A heavy blow thrown straight after the Stormguard or a parry becomes a full circle that no guard stops and that reaches every man around you. |
| **Storm-Cry** | 6 | 35 | 2 | A shout with thunder under it. Enemies within nine paces lose the blow they were about to throw, the crew take heart and mend, and Eirik's own anger goes out of him. |
| **Stormbreak** | 7 | 100 | 3 | Spend the whole bar. For eight breaths every blow hits a third harder and throws a spark of lightning into a second man. |

## The Wolf's table: each champion's weapon and skills

Fought in the order they fall in the story, each in the place the story has him fall. A skill's name shows over the champion's guard when he uses it.

### 1. Arne Longshot — first of the seven

*Act III. The watchtower, Jarnøy.* Health 220.

- **Black-Feather Volley.** Every third draw is three arrows, one after another.
- **Pinning Shot.** An aimed arrow that slows whoever it hits.
- **Cornered Cat.** Up close: seax cuts, then away with a pair of thrown knives.
- *Left behind:* a wolf brooch.

### 2. Rurik Shieldsplitter — second of the seven

*Act IV. His longship, off Solvik.* Health 300.

- **Shield-Split.** An overhead that splits a shield that catches it and empties the guard.
- **The Even Hand.** He turns a blow on his own shield and answers at once.
- **His rule.** He will not strike a man who is down, disarmed or out of breath. He waits.
- *Left behind:* Rurik's sword, and a wolf brooch.

### 3. Skarde Bear-Maw — third of the seven

*Act V. The holmgang ground, Vængr.* Health 380, armour (light blows count x0.75), tires.

- **The Lie.** He says he was at the farm in the snow. Words: they make Eirik too angry for the Stormguard, unless his guard is up.
- **Bear's Maw.** A leaping overhead that no guard stops and that splits a shield.
- **Shield-Biter.** A sweep the whole way round.
- **He tires.** Every blow costs him wind. Give ground, let him swing at air, and he slows.
- *Left behind:* Skarde's Dane axe, and a wolf brooch.

### 4. Eydis the Mire — fourth of the seven

*Act VI. The drowned watchtower, Myrholm.* Health 260.

- **Mire-Lance.** She keeps him at the end of a long spear and backs away when he closes.
- **Poisoned Point.** A thrust that leaves poison in the wound.
- **Javelin Rain.** Three javelins from the bundle on her back.
- **Sucking Ground.** Stake pits wait under the water, and she fouls the mud under his feet.
- *Left behind:* Eydis's spear, and a wolf brooch.

### 5. Bersi Black-Anvil — fifth of the seven

*Act VI. The forge street, Skalløy.* Health 480, armour (light blows count x0.55).

- **Anvil-Drop.** The hammer into the ground: everyone near it, and no guard helps.
- **Shield-Breaker.** An overhead that splits a shield and empties the guard behind it.
- **Horse-Killer.** A charge from across the street.
- **He does not tire.** But all his weight is in every blow. Make one land on nothing and he is open.
- *Left behind:* Black-Anvil's hammer, and a wolf brooch.

### 6. Ulf Two-Blades — sixth of the seven

*Act VII. The lashed ships, Svartøy harbour.* Health 340.

- **The Scissor.** Both swords at once, from both sides. Only a shield stops it.
- **Whirl.** Four cuts without a breath between them.
- **Change-Step.** He changes direction in the middle of a swing and is at your side.
- *Left behind:* Ulf's two swords, and a wolf brooch.

### 7. Kettil Red-Hand — last of the seven

*Act VII. The bend in the road, Svartøy.* Health 280.

- **Buckler-Hook.** A twist of the buckler takes the weapon out of Eirik's hand for a few breaths, even through a guard.
- **Mud in the Eyes.** Thrown from three paces. A raised guard keeps it out.
- **Boot-Knife.** A feint with the axe, then the knife from his boot, which no guard stops.
- *Left behind:* a wolf brooch.

### 8. Vigdis Quickblood — the Left Hand

*Act VII. The long bridge, Svartøy.* Health 300.

- **Left-Left, Right-Right.** She steps twice and strikes, always from the side: two blows from the left, then two from the right. Listen for her feet; raise the guard on the beat.
- **Quickblood.** Now and then she is behind him instead.
- **Too fast to chase.** She is gone the moment her blow is thrown. Two blows turned on the beat break her guard.
- *Left behind:* Vigdis's seaxes.

### 9. Gorm Stone-Lung — the Right Hand

*Act VII. The gate of Håkon's hall, Svartøy.* Health 560, armour (light blows count x0.4), tires.

- **Wall of Iron.** Three layers of mail and an iron-bound shield: nothing gets through from the front until the rim is broken.
- **Shield-Swat.** A swing of the shield that no guard stops and that takes the breath out of him.
- **Stone-Lung Crush.** The club into the ground, hard enough to turn a stone step to gravel.
- **He tires, slowly.** Make him swing. When the shield is gone he is faster, and open.

### 10. Jarl Håkon Ironwolf — the Ironwolf

*Act VII. The Hall of Wolves, Svartøy.* Health 760.

- **The sword of the guardsman.** Sword and shield, every block clean, every counter punishing. He reads a blow before it begins.
- **The weapons of war.** Spear, then axe, then sword again from the racks, changing whenever Eirik gets used to one; a bench at his legs, the hearth's coals in his face.
- **Wolf's Judgment.** The spiked hammer. It breaks a shield to pieces and no guard stops it.
- **The last oath.** The scar goes cold: no Stormguard, no storm skills. Stop attacking. Wait. Answer.
